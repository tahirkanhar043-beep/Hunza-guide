People and culture

Traditions that shape daily life in the valley.

Community

Hunza has a high literacy rate and a strong tradition of local schools and community groups. Most families farm small terraced fields.

Festivals

  • Ginani celebrates the harvest in summer.
  • Navroz marks the new year in March with food and music.
  • Polo matches are held on village grounds in the cooler months.

Visiting respectfully

Dress modestly, ask before taking photos of people, and remove shoes when entering homes.
