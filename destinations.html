Places to visit

Five stops, from forts to glacier lakes.

Baltit Fort

A restored fort above Karimabad with a view over the whole valley.

Half day

Altit Fort

The older of the two forts, with narrow lanes in the village below.

2 hours

Attabad Lake

A turquoise lake formed by a landslide in 2010. Boat rides run along it.

Half day

Passu Cones

Sharp peaks beside the Passu Glacier. Good for a short walk.

3 hours

Khunjerab Pass

The high border road to China, at about 4,700 metres.

Full day
