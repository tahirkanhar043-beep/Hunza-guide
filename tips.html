Travel tips

Practical advice before you go.

Getting there

  • Fly to Gilgit, then drive about 3 hours.
  • Or take the Karakoram Highway from Islamabad.

What to pack

  • Layers for cold nights
  • Sun protection
  • Sturdy walking shoes

Stay safe

  • Check road and weather news first.
  • Allow a day to adjust to altitude.
