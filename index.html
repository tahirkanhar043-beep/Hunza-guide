Meet the Hunza Valley

A mountain valley in northern Pakistan with glaciers, apricot orchards and the Karakoram peaks overhead.

See the 7-day plan

Why go

Karakoram views

Rakaposhi and Ultar Sar stand over the valley, visible from most villages.

Apricot season

Blossoms open in April and fruit ripens in July. Dried apricots are sold year round.

Old forts

Baltit and Altit forts are centuries old and open to visitors.
