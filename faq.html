When is the best time to visit?

April to October. Spring has blossoms and autumn has golden leaves.

Do I need a permit?

Rules change, so check current requirements before you travel.

Is there mobile coverage?

Coverage is available in towns and weak in remote areas.

How many days do I need?

Five to seven days covers the main places.
